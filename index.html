Our Heritage

The Aura Story

A Legacy of
Master Craftsmanship

Founded in 1982 by Eleanor Vance, Aura Textiles began as a small, independent weaving mill in the heart of the Canadian Rockies. Driven by a passion for the tactile beauty of natural fibers, Eleanor set out to create textiles that would stand the test of time, both in durability and design.

Today, we continue that legacy. Our process is a delicate dance between honoring centuries-old weaving traditions and embracing modern, sustainable innovations. Every yard of silk, every spool of cashmere, and every bolt of wool that leaves our mill has been subjected to rigorous quality control by masterful hands.

We don't just manufacture fabric; we craft the canvas for your most inspired creations. From the sourcing of ethically produced raw materials to the final inspection in our Calgary facility, every step is infused with intention and artistry.

Eleanor Vance, Founder
